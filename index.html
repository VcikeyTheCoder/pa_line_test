    How to read the print

    Each bar is printed slow, then fast, then slow, at the PA value printed next to it. Look at the top layer under a light from the side.

    • Left end, where it speeds up: push. PA too low shows a thin spot right after the change, too high shows a bump. Pick the bar that stays even.
    • Right end, where it slows down: pull. PA too low shows a bulge just before the change, too high shows a thin spot. Pick the bar that stays even.

    Push and pull can come from different bars. Put them in [dual_pa] as push_pressure_advance and pull_pressure_advance. If they are the same, just use that as your normal pressure advance.
